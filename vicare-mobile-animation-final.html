About Dr. Juhi · Founder & lead doctor

Dr. Juhi is the founder and lead doctor at ViCare, dedicated to providing personalized skin, hair and aesthetic care. With a patient-first approach, she combines medical expertise, advanced technologies and modern aesthetic treatments to create natural-looking, personalized results.

Her approach focuses on understanding each patient’s individual concerns, developing tailored treatment plans, and prioritizing safety, comfort and long-term skin and hair health.
